# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Healthcare professionals (doctors, pharmacists) verifying prescribing information; international partners and importers evaluating manufacturing credibility; investors and stakeholders reviewing governance and financials; job seekers assessing the employer; patients seeking trust signals. They arrive on desktop browsers during business hours, often on office connections, scanning for proof of quality, scale, and legitimacy.

## Product Purpose

Corporate website for Popular Pharmaceuticals PLC, a top-15 Bangladeshi branded-generics manufacturer and the country's pioneer Human Insulin producer. The site must establish institutional credibility, showcase the product portfolio and cGMP manufacturing capabilities, present the export network, and route each audience (HCPs, partners, investors, candidates) to their next step. Success: a visitor concludes within seconds that this is a legitimate, regulated, serious pharmaceutical company.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, credible. Institutional trust in the lane of Novo Nordisk and GSK: confident restraint, exact grids, disciplined spacing, photography and real data doing the persuasion. Never loud, never playful, never decorative for its own sake.

## Anti-references

Generic AI-generated marketing sites: rounded-2xl card grids with icon chips, gradient text and gradient buttons, floating blur blobs, decorative rings and dotted patterns, oversized display type, SaaS hero-metric blocks, glassmorphism. Anything that reads as a template rather than an institution.

## Design Principles

- Evidence over assertion: real photography, real numbers, real certifications carry the message; decoration never substitutes for proof.
- Quiet authority: fewer, larger, well-aligned elements; whitespace is deliberate, never default.
- One system, no exceptions: consistent radii, spacing, type scale, and component behavior across every page.
- Information density where it serves experts: product and prescribing content stays compact and scannable.
- Accessibility is table stakes: WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, keyboard paths, visible focus.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: 4.5:1 body text contrast (3:1 large text), full keyboard navigability, visible focus states, descriptive alt text, reduced-motion respect for carousels and map animation.
